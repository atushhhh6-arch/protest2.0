    07 / FAIR QUESTIONS

    Clear bids.
    Clear rules.

    How much does a spot start at?

    On both front and back, two upper positions start at $200 each, the centre rectangle at $350 and two lower positions at $150 each. Two sleeve positions start at $100 each and are shown only on the front; the back has no duplicate sleeve spots. All amounts are USD.

    Can I pay more than the minimum?

    Yes. You can choose any valid amount at or above the current minimum. The next takeover minimum is exactly 2× your actual paid sponsorship. Pay $500, and the next sponsor must pay at least $1,000.

    What happens when someone takes my spot?

    After their qualifying payment succeeds, their brand replaces yours and you become eligible for a full refund of your original sponsorship payment. Your old amount is removed from the current sponsor total.

    When is my spot confirmed?

    Only after a successful payment and a booking confirmation. Browsing a position or preparing your logo does not reserve it. Payment checkout is currently closed.

    How is the sponsor total calculated?

    Only the current winning payment for each spot is counted. If a $200 sponsor is replaced by a $500 sponsor, the total for that position becomes $500—not $700.

    When does bidding close and printing happen?

    A print-lock deadline has not been announced. The deadline and final attendance details must be published before live checkout opens. Once a position is locked for printing, further takeovers for that print run close.

    Will the T-shirt be worn until the protest ends?

    That is Ayush’s intention on the days he attends. Attendance depends on access and conditions; no uninterrupted stay or fixed protest duration is guaranteed.

    What if the project cannot go ahead?

    If the promised placement cannot be delivered because the project is cancelled before fulfilment, the affected sponsorship is eligible for a full refund. Any alternative date or deliverable requires the sponsor’s agreement. See the Refund Policy for details.

    Are views or media appearances guaranteed?

    No. Historical reach and crowds are context only. There is no guaranteed audience, viral reach, television appearance, sales result or endorsement by protest organisers.

    What logos and brands are allowed?

    Use artwork you own or have permission to use. We do not accept illegal, deceptive, hateful, violent, sexually explicit or impersonating content. See the Content Policy. An unsuitable logo must be replaced before printing.
