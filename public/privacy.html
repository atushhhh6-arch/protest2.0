1. Information entered on this website

You can enter a brand name, description, website, X handle and proposed bid, and select a logo file. With checkout closed, these details are not submitted to Ayush or a sponsorship database. Applying a brand in Moderator preview saves its details and cropped logo in this browser so you can test placements. No payment credentials, billing address, identity document or email address are requested by this version.

2. Logo preparation

Your selected image is read and cropped inside your browser. The image and crop are not uploaded. Applying a brand in Moderator preview saves the cropped image locally in this browser; it can survive a refresh. An image selected without applying a preview remains only in page memory. Use only artwork you are authorised to provide, and avoid personal or sensitive information in brand descriptions and images.

3. Hosting, fonts and external links

The hosting and sign-in service may process request, device or account information under its own privacy terms. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive ordinary connection details such as an IP address. No additional advertising pixel or analytics script is configured in this version.

Opening X or another external website connects to that service and is governed by its privacy practices. The brand form is not submitted by an external link. Do not put passwords or secret access tokens in website links.

4. Device storage and retention

Moderator preview stores test brand details, cropped logos, selected spots, example amounts, preview mode and up to 50 local placement events in this browser. This is device-local testing data, not a shared sponsor record. Reset saved profile clears form details; Reset selected spot or Reset all spots clears the relevant placements and history; Reset everything removes this version’s preview storage. Exiting preview hides test brands while keeping them saved. You can also remove all versions’ local data in your browser settings. Clearing one browser does not clear another device or external service. Anyone using this browser profile may be able to view its saved previews.

5. Sponsorship data when booking opens

Payment checkout is currently closed. Before it opens, this policy must identify the payment provider and the collection, storage, retention and support processes for shared sponsor accounts, bookings, receipts and refunds.

A confirmed sponsor’s approved name, logo, description, website, X handle, paid sponsorship amount and selected position are intended for public display. Private payment credentials, billing details and support correspondence must not appear on public profiles.
