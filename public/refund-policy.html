Refund Policy

Test-mode checkout is active. Test payments do not move real money. A live position is confirmed only after a successful live payment and booking confirmation.

1. Refunds apply to completed payments

Opening a placement, preparing a logo or entering an amount does not move money. A refund is owed only against an actual eligible completed payment. Test-mode transactions are for verification only and do not move real money.

2. Full original checkout refund when your spot is taken over

Once another sponsor’s qualifying payment successfully takes over your position, the previous sponsor is eligible for a full refund of the original Dodo Payments checkout transaction for that position. Where Dodo included tax such as GST in the original charge and returns that tax as part of a full refund, that tax is included in the refund.

Example: if your sponsorship amount is $250 and Dodo charges additional tax at checkout, an eligible takeover refund is initiated against the full original Dodo payment, not only the $250 sponsorship amount. The replacement sponsor’s sponsorship amount still determines the next takeover minimum.

Protest 2.0 does not deduct its own printing or project costs from an eligible takeover refund. Bank fees, card-network charges, foreign-exchange differences or other amounts imposed outside Dodo Payments may still be outside our control and can affect what appears on the customer’s statement.

3. Refund status and timing

Eligibility, initiation and completion are separate events. A confirmed takeover creates the refund entitlement. Refunds may be initiated automatically by the project backend or manually by Ayush through Dodo Payments to the original payment method. A refund is complete only after Dodo Payments confirms it as succeeded.

Refunds may take time to appear because processing depends on the payment provider, bank and payment method. There is no guaranteed instant or fixed banking timeline. If your refund is taking longer than expected, contact Ayush with your receipt or transaction reference and selected spot ID so the status can be checked and resolved.

4. Cancellation or inability to deliver

If the project is cancelled before fulfilment, or an affected sponsor’s promised placement cannot be delivered, the affected sponsor is eligible for a refund of the eligible original Dodo checkout payment. Where Dodo returns tax as part of that full refund, the tax is included. Any substitute date or content arrangement requires that sponsor’s agreement. If a sponsor wants to cancel for another reason, contact Ayush before printing; mandatory rights under applicable law are preserved.

5. Failed, duplicate or conflicting purchases

A failed payment does not create ownership. A duplicate charge or a successful payment that cannot secure the selected position must be investigated and refunded where no agreed service is supplied. Changing your mind does not itself count as a takeover; a qualifying replacement must actually complete the takeover.

6. Artwork rejected before fulfilment

If proposed artwork does not meet the Content Policy, the sponsor will be asked to provide suitable replacement artwork. If the sponsorship is declined before fulfilment, the sponsorship/spot amount paid will be refunded. Taxes and other third-party charges are handled separately by the relevant payment provider unless applicable law requires otherwise. A rejected brand must not remain labelled as an active sponsor.

7. Request help

Keep your receipt or transaction reference and selected spot ID. If a refund appears delayed, fails, or you have not received an expected update, contact Ayush using the project contact below and include those details so the refund can be checked manually. Never send card numbers, passwords or one-time codes. A valid refund remains owed even if the payment provider or bank takes additional time to complete it.
