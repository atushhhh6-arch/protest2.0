1. Refunds apply to completed payments

Opening a placement, preparing a logo or entering an amount does not move money. A refund is owed only against an actual eligible payment. Checkout is currently closed and no payment can be made here.

2. Sponsorship amount refund when your spot is taken over

Once another sponsor’s qualifying payment successfully takes over your position, you are entitled to a refund of the sponsorship/spot amount you paid for that position. The refund is based on the spot amount recorded by Protest 2.0, not necessarily the total amount charged at checkout if taxes or other third-party charges were added separately.

Example: if your spot amount is $250 and another sponsor later pays $600, your refundable sponsorship amount is $250. The current winning spot amount becomes $600, and the next minimum becomes $1,200.

Taxes, payment-provider charges, bank fees, currency-conversion differences and other third-party charges are not part of the sponsorship/spot refund unless they must be refunded under applicable law or are separately returned by the relevant payment provider. Protest 2.0 does not deduct its own printing or project costs from the refundable sponsorship/spot amount.

3. Refund status and timing

Eligibility, initiation and completion are separate events. A confirmed takeover creates the refund entitlement. Refunds are reviewed and initiated manually by Ayush through the connected payment provider to the original payment method. A refund is complete only after the payment provider confirms it.

Refunds may take time to appear because processing depends on the payment provider, bank and payment method. There is no guaranteed instant or fixed banking timeline. If your refund is taking longer than expected, contact Ayush with your receipt or transaction reference and selected spot ID so the status can be checked and resolved.

4. Cancellation or inability to deliver

If the project is cancelled before fulfilment, or an affected sponsor’s promised placement cannot be delivered, the affected sponsor is eligible for a refund of the sponsorship/spot amount paid. Taxes and other third-party charges are handled separately by the relevant payment provider unless applicable law requires otherwise. Any substitute date or content arrangement requires that sponsor’s agreement. If a sponsor wants to cancel for another reason, contact Ayush before printing; mandatory rights under applicable law are preserved.

5. Failed, duplicate or conflicting purchases

A failed payment does not create ownership. A duplicate charge or a successful payment that cannot secure the selected position must be investigated and refunded where no agreed service is supplied. Changing your mind does not itself count as a takeover; a qualifying replacement must actually complete the takeover.

6. Artwork rejected before fulfilment

If proposed artwork does not meet the Content Policy, the sponsor will be asked to provide suitable replacement artwork. If the sponsorship is declined before fulfilment, the sponsorship/spot amount paid will be refunded. Taxes and other third-party charges are handled separately by the relevant payment provider unless applicable law requires otherwise. A rejected brand must not remain labelled as an active sponsor.

7. Request help

Keep your receipt or transaction reference and selected spot ID. If a refund appears delayed, fails, or you have not received an expected update, contact Ayush using the project contact below and include those details so the refund can be checked manually. Never send card numbers, passwords or one-time codes. A valid refund remains owed even if the payment provider or bank takes additional time to complete it.
