1. Refunds apply to completed payments

Opening a placement, preparing a logo or entering an amount does not move money. A refund is owed only against an actual eligible payment. Checkout is currently closed and no payment can be made here.

2. Full refund when your spot is taken over

Once another sponsor’s qualifying payment successfully takes over your position, you are entitled to a full refund of the sponsorship amount you paid for that position. You are not refunded the new sponsor’s bid or the next minimum—only your own original paid amount.

Example: you pay $250 and another sponsor later pays $600. Their payment qualifies because it exceeds the $500 minimum. Your $250 is refundable in full. The current winning amount becomes $600, and the next minimum becomes $1,200.

The project will not deduct its printing, platform or processing costs from the full sponsorship refund owed for a takeover. Third-party bank charges or currency-conversion differences outside the sponsorship payment may depend on the payment method; any relevant currency terms must be shown before live checkout.

3. Refund status and timing

Eligibility, initiation and completion are separate events. A confirmed takeover creates the refund entitlement. The operator then issues the refund through the connected payment provider to the original payment method. It is complete only after provider confirmation. Pending, failed or review states must be tracked and resolved rather than described as completed.

No instant refund or fixed banking timeline is promised. Processing depends on the provider and bank. Real refund automation is not connected on this website; before launch, the operator must establish a working refund process and maintain enough funds to honour all refunds owed.

4. Cancellation or inability to deliver

If the project is cancelled before fulfilment, or an affected sponsor’s promised placement cannot be delivered, the affected sponsorship is eligible for a full refund. Any substitute date or content arrangement requires that sponsor’s agreement. If a sponsor wants to cancel for another reason, contact Ayush before printing; mandatory rights under applicable law are preserved.

5. Failed, duplicate or conflicting purchases

A failed payment does not create ownership. A duplicate charge or a successful payment that cannot secure the selected position must be investigated and refunded where no agreed service is supplied. Changing your mind does not itself count as a takeover; a qualifying replacement must actually complete the takeover.

6. Artwork rejected before fulfilment

If proposed artwork does not meet the Content Policy, the sponsor will be asked to provide suitable replacement artwork. If the sponsorship is declined before fulfilment, the paid sponsorship will be refunded in full. A rejected brand must not remain labelled as an active sponsor.

7. Request help

When real checkout becomes available, retain the receipt or transaction reference and selected spot ID. Send those details through the published support channel when requesting help. Never send card numbers, passwords or one-time codes. Unresolved provider failures do not remove the project’s responsibility to resolve a valid refund.
